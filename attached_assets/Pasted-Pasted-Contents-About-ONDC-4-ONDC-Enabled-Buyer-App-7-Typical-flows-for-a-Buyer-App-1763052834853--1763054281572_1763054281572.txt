CHAPTER 1: Introduction to ONDC
About ONDC
Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC) aims to democratize digital commerce by enabling an interoperable framework, for different demand side entities (also called buyer network participant or buyer NP) & supply side entities (also called seller network participant or seller NP) to transact, while providing the convenience and quality of service that is available in digital commerce today.
ONDC participants, on the demand & supply side, define their terms & conditions and dynamically enter into a contractual relationship, by signing a digital contract, to facilitate a transaction between the buyer and seller.

CHAPTER 2- Customizing Saarthi
Technology Stack
Saarthi has been developed using React Native for mobile, with a backend powered by Node.js and Python. It provides plug-n-play integration with services for payment, maps, etc. with default out-of-the-box configurations available for Razorpay (payment), Bhashini (language support), email/SMS integration, and Mappls (maps), Odoo (order management).
